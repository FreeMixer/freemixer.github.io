The MOD GUI generator

tools/modgui-gen.mjs writes a MOD GUI (MOD Devices' modgui, the format MOD and Zynthian draw) for one plugin, from its descriptor (omx-contract docs/descriptors.md) and, when the plugin declares one, its panel.

omx-panel modgui --descriptor <file> | --installed <package>/<name>
                 [--panel <file>] --out <bundle.lv2> [--stem <name>] [--source <text>] [--easy-first] [--check | --emit-stdout]
  • --descriptor names a descriptor file; --installed omx-plugins/omx-delay reads the one a package installed into omx-contract's contract directory (<contractdir>/3/<package>/plugins/<name>.json).
  • --panel names a JSON file holding the declared panel: family, sections (each key, label, controls by symbol) and, optionally, widgets (symbol to knob, fader, switch or selector, the list in schema/panel.schema.json) and easy (the controls of the Easy face). With none, the sections are the descriptor's groups, laid out by tools/panel-description.mjs, and the family the one their meanings make.
  • --out is the LV2 bundle the files go into: modgui.ttl and modgui/. --stem names the files (icon-<stem>.html, stylesheet-<stem>.css, screenshot-<stem>.png, thumbnail-<stem>.png); it defaults to the bundle's name without .lv2.
  • --source is the text the files' banner names as what they were made from; it defaults to the descriptor and the panel files.
  • --check writes nothing and refuses a stale, missing or extra file byte for byte; --emit-stdout prints every text file and one digest line per image.

Every control's symbol, travel, default and label is the descriptor's. A toggle is a switch; a choice, or an integer travel of at most 16 steps, a selector over its values; every other travel a knob. The template lays out every section and every control; modgui.ttl lists the input controls of the first section as modgui:port. With --easy-first the Easy controls (panel-layout.md) are that first section, so a pedal's listed ports are them; a plugin's package turns it on when it regenerates its committed files, and omx-modgui-sync always does.

A readout (a readonly control: a gain reduction, a level) has no travel to turn and no default. It is drawn as a display: a frame in its cell carrying its symbol and its range, never a knob, a fader, a switch or a selector, and never looked up as a control input. MOD's template binds input control ports by role (input-control-port) and has no role for an output control port, so the frame carries none; it is not a modgui:port either. Showing the reading live needs the bundle's own modgui:javascript, which this generator does not write. A declared panel that names a widget for a readout is refused.

The images (the screenshot, the thumbnail, the knob film and the fader's when a control is one) are rasterised by tools/raster.mjs from the template's own layout, each control at its default. The screenshot and the thumbnail carry the plugin title, the section labels (one band per section, so per group of a grouped descriptor), and each control's name and its default with unit; the thumbnail is the screenshot at half size. The text is set from the font file the look names (--font-file, tools/fonts/Inter-Regular.ttf, SIL OFL 1.1, licence beside it), whose outlines tools/font.mjs reads and tools/raster.mjs fills at the shapes' own sampling: no host font is consulted. A fixed-code deflate and no zlib, so their bytes depend only on the descriptor, the panel and the look.

The look

tools/look/tokens.css is the omx look's token cascade, the one source of the colours and the font a renderer wears. tools/look.mjs resolves it at the look it names into tools/look.json, every colour a plain #rrggbb; omx-panel look --check refuses a look.json that is not what the cascade resolves to.

The test

tools/modgui-test.sh <bundle.lv2> <descriptor> [<panel>] holds a written MOD GUI to its descriptor on values: the files are the generation byte for byte; every control is drawn in the template with its symbol, travel and default (a readout with its symbol and range, as a display with no port role), and nothing undeclared is; every choice is a selector with each of its values; sord_validate accepts the bundle against the LV2 specifications, tools/modgui-terms.ttl (the closed modgui vocabulary) and omx-contract's resource-hint terms, and refuses a property outside that vocabulary; and a descriptor with its first control's default moved makes --check fail on the template.
